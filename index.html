RUMOR BIN / SOURCE HYGIENE · CORRECTIONNov 20, 2026

Not every rumor belongs in the rumor bin forever

What changed: One roundup labeled Nvidia's proposed Hugging Face purchase an unconfirmed rumor. Reuters had already reported the $12.9 billion acquisition agreement in September, and Nvidia subsequently referenced it as pending in financial disclosures.

Status: Announced/pending acquisition is not the same thing as a completed purchase. A separate suggestion that Nvidia would halt all China chip sales needs specific sourcing before publication as fact.

Why keep it: A useful example of how a stale aggregator headline can make documented news look speculative.

Source: Reuters · Sep 3 ↗

We separate documented events from interpretations and rumors. A mistake is not the same as a shutdown.
